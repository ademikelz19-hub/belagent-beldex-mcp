03 / POLICY & TRANSPARENCY

Demo vs. live

Demo mode simulates the approval flow without touching a blockchain. Testnet mode invokes your installed Beldex browser wallet, strictly requiring manual confirmation.

A wallet-returned transaction hash is labeled submitted_unverified until independently verified. BelAgent never fabricates on-chain settlement receipts.
